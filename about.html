About Raja Software Solution

A mobile game development and publishing studio from Khanewal, Pakistan, building games for players worldwide.

Who We Are

Mobile Games Are What We Do

Raja Software Solution is a mobile game development and software publishing business based in Khanewal, Punjab, Pakistan. Our work is fully focused on mobile: we design, build, publish and maintain 3D games made for phones and tablets.

We specialize in stealth, strategy, action and simulation games: genres where clever level design, responsive touch controls and steady difficulty curves matter most. Every game we make is built to be easy to pick up, hard to put down, and free to download.

The studio is led by its CEO and founder, Atiq ur Rehman, who also publishes our games on the Apple App Store under his developer account.

Our games are already published and live on the Apple App Store for iPhone and iPad. Our next step is bringing them, and every future title, to Android players through the Samsung Galaxy Store.

How We Work

Our Game Development Process

Every title goes through the same complete process, from first idea to live updates.

01

Concept & Research

We study what mobile players enjoy, choose a genre and core gameplay loop, and plan missions, levels and progression.

02

Game Design

We design levels, controls, UI and difficulty curves around touchscreens, so the game feels natural on phones and tablets.

03

3D Development

We build 3D environments, characters, AI behaviour and game systems such as stealth detection, crafting and upgrades.

04

Optimization

We tune performance, file size and battery use so games run smoothly on a wide range of devices, not just flagship models.

05

Testing & QA

We test on real devices, fix bugs and check every build against each store's content and technical guidelines before release.

06

Publishing & Updates

We publish, monitor reviews and crash reports, and ship regular updates with fixes and improvements.
