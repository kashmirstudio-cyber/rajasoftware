Privacy Policy

How Raja Software Solutions collects, uses and protects information in our games and website.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Raja Software Solutions ("we", "us", "our") develops and publishes mobile games, including Escape Prison Jail Simulator, Robbery Simulator Thief Heist (together, the "Games"). This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our Games or visit this website, and how that information is used.

By downloading, installing or using our Games, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, address, email or phone number inside our Games.

Our Games may use third-party services that automatically collect limited information, including:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising identifier (IDFA) or other device IDs;
  • Usage data such as how you interact with the Game, sessions, levels played and advertising interactions;
  • Technical information such as device model, operating system version, language, and crash or performance diagnostics.

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the Games;
  • To show advertisements, including personalized ads where you have allowed it;
  • To measure ad performance and understand how players use the Games;
  • To find and fix bugs, crashes and performance problems.

4. Advertising and Tracking

Our Games are free and are supported by advertising. We work with third-party advertising and analytics partners who may use device identifiers and usage data to show ads and measure their effectiveness, including across apps and websites owned by other companies.

On iOS 14.5 and later, the Game will ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before tracking you. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

5. Third-Party Services

Third-party services used in our Games (such as advertising networks, analytics tools and game engines) have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to read them. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties.

6. In-App Purchases

If a Game offers in-app purchases, payments are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Games are rated 13+ on the App Store and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Data Retention and Security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described above. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Your Choices and Rights

  • Turn off ad tracking at any time in your device settings;
  • Reset your advertising identifier in your device settings;
  • Stop all collection by uninstalling the Game;
  • Contact us to ask questions or request deletion of any data we may hold about you.

10. This Website

This website does not use cookies for tracking and does not collect personal information. If you contact us through the website, your message is sent through WhatsApp or your phone, and we only use it to reply to you.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
