About the Game

Robbery Simulator Thief Heist is a stealth robbery game where planning, timing and skill decide whether you succeed. Start with small break-ins and work your way up to high-risk jobs with tougher security.

Every mission is about smart decisions: choose your path, avoid detection and escape before anyone catches on.

Key Features

  • Stealth heists: Sneak past cameras, alarms and other high-security systems.
  • Growing missions: From small break-ins to big, high-risk robbery jobs.
  • Strategy first: Plan routes and make decisions that change how each job plays out.
  • Rising challenge: Difficulty increases level by level to keep you on your toes.
