About the Game

Escape Prison Jail Simulator puts you behind bars in a high-security prison where every move counts. Watch how the guards patrol, collect and craft useful items, and decide whether to slip out silently or force your way through in an action-packed breakout.

Each level mixes stealth, timing and strategy, so no two escapes play out quite the same way.

Key Features

  • Escape missions: Challenging levels with smart guards and dynamic obstacles.
  • Many ways out: Dig tunnels, distract guards, hide in the shadows or create diversions.
  • Reactive guard AI: Guards respond to noise and suspicious activity, so stay careful.
  • Crafting & upgrades: Build tools from what you find and improve your abilities.
  • Big environments: Prison yards, tunnels, cell blocks, rooftops and restricted zones.
  • Multiple modes: Stealth missions, action breakouts and timing-based challenge levels.
